Hi! I am a Master of Engineering student in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST), Nanjing, China. I am a Research Assistant at the School of Computer Science and Software. Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology from Jiangxi University of Technology.

Research

My research interests lie in Computer Vision, Image Segmentation, Vision Transformers, and Attention Mechanisms. I focus on Medical Image Analysis, Remote Sensing Image Segmentation, Neural Architecture Search, Multimodal and Foundation Models, and Deep Learning for Healthcare Prediction. I design deep learning models, including Transformer-, attention-, and Mamba-based architectures, for medical and remote sensing image segmentation.
