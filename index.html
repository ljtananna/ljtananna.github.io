Hi! I am a Master of Engineering student in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST), Nanjing, China. Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology from Jiangxi University of Technology.

Research

My research interests include Computer Vision and Image Segmentation, Vision Transformers and Attention Mechanisms, Medical Image Analysis, Remote Sensing Image Segmentation, Neural Architecture Search, Multimodal and Foundation Models, Deep Learning for Healthcare Prediction, and Machine Learning and Optimization.

I build efficient, adaptable computer vision systems for medical imaging and remote sensing, domains where labeled data is scarce and imaging conditions constantly shift across scanners, modalities, and geographies. My work follows two directions: designing data-adaptive architectures for semantic segmentation, deformable attention for object detection [DANet'23], and transformer-based deep fusion for confocal microendoscopy segmentation (UperNet-ViT), and adapting vision foundation models for specialized domains under few-shot and limited-supervision settings, including frequency-guided Mamba decoding (FreCaM) and DINOv3-based multi-scale segmentation (UNet-MSMamba) for remote sensing, MRI-based bone age prediction, and instance segmentation for autonomous driving [ICCIT'25].
